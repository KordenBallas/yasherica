What was built

Detached stage (the core "UI layer only" requirement). The pot no longer lives on the Hero. A new InventoryStage.prefab (parked at world y = −50, everything on layer 8 InventoryFocus) holds the cauldron, liquid, steam, bubble container, crafting slots, the success/fail puffs, a stage-only light, and its own perspective camera. That camera is a URP overlay camera stacked on the Main Camera, so the cauldron renders screen-centered over the world while the hero stays visible behind it via the base camera's belly zoom. The PhysicsRaycaster moved from the Main Camera to the stage camera — when the stage camera is disabled (inventory closed), bubble clicks are automatically inert.

Character rotation. New ICharacterFacing + CharacterFacingController on the Hero root: smoothstep yaw toward ICameraService.OutputCameraPosition on open, restore on close. CameraService now glues the belly Cinemachine camera to the hero's BellyCameraAnchor every frame while the belly view is active, so the zoom lands correctly while the hero rotates underneath it.

Cross-section cauldron. CauldronProfileCalculator (pure C#) computes paired outer/inner lathe polylines — sphere-slice belly, flat floor slab, rim lip — and CauldronMeshBuilder sweeps them 200° around Y with flat shading, capping the cut planes so the open front reads as solid wall. Generated at runtime by CauldronView, tunable in the inspector.

Boiling liquid + steam. LiquidWaveCalculator (pure C#, two crossed traveling sine waves) drives per-vertex displacement of a generated pie-sector mesh in LiquidSurfaceView, with a pulsing emission glow (the _EMISSION keyword was already enabled on M_PotLiquid). A looping steam plume plays while the pot is focused, surface bubbles rise continuously, and the craft puffs were retuned as steam bursts (bright/warm for success, dark for failure).

Pseudo-3D items. BubbleView now stacks N alpha-clipped quad layers per artifact (default 4), receding and darkening toward the back, sharing one material so SRP batching holds. Layer count/spacing/darkening live in InventoryConfig alongside the new liquid and facing tunables.

What changed

1. Clicks — replaced the UGUI raycaster with an explicit one. Every part of the PhysicsRaycaster setup checked out statically (masks, EventSystem, colliders, presenter wiring, even the package source), which pins the failure on the known-fragile combination of UGUI raycasting through a URP overlay camera. The new StageClickRouter on the StageCamera raycasts from the stage camera itself on pointer press (Input System), skips clicks that land on HUD elements, and forwards hits via the new BubbleView.NotifyClicked() — so pot bubbles and the crafted result both work through the existing event chain. IPointerClickHandler and the PhysicsRaycaster are removed, leaving a single deterministic click path that's automatically inert while the stage camera is disabled.

2. Transparency — a real bug. The shell material was already transparent (alpha 0.25), but BubbleView was writing the artifact tint — alpha 1 in all seven definitions — into the shell's _BaseColor property block, overriding the material. The shell now keeps the tint's hue but forces a serialized _shellAlpha (0.25, tunable on Bubble.prefab), so the artifact inside is visible.

3. Depth — bubbles now spread in 3D. BubblePlacement gained a Z component and BubbleLayoutCalculator distributes depth deterministically with a plastic-ratio sequence (decorrelated from the golden-angle spiral), bounded by a new _potInteriorHalfDepth (0.12) on PotView. Under the perspective stage camera, near bubbles render larger and overlap far ones. Three new tests cover depth bounds, variation, and the flat-at-zero case.

4. Framing. Belly zoom: the Main Camera is orthographic, so the only knob that matters is the BellyCamera Cinemachine lens — OrthographicSize 1 → 0.65 (~35% tighter on the character). Pot size: StageCamera moved from z −2 to −1.6 (~25% bigger on screen); I checked the crafting result anchor still fits in frame at the new distance.

Docs (inventory-subsystem.md) are updated, including new requirement R11a documenting why the stage uses explicit raycasting.
